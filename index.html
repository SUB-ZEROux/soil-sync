Crop Growth & Soil Types Information

Wheat Growth Cycle

Wheat requires well-drained loamy soil and moderate rainfall. The growth cycle includes germination, tillering, stem elongation, heading, and ripening. Understanding each phase is crucial for optimal yield and quality harvest.

Soil Types Overview

Soil types vary from sandy, clay, silt, peat, chalk, to loam. Each type affects water retention, nutrient availability, and crop suitability. Learn how to identify and optimize your soil for better harvests.
