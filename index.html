Wheat Growth Cycle

Wheat needs balanced moisture, healthy soil structure, and good timing to move through germination, tillering, heading, and grain filling.

Soil Types Overview

Loamy soil gives the best balance of drainage, moisture retention, and nutrients. Sandy, clay, and silt soils each need different care practices.
